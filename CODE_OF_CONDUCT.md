# Code of Conduct

CesiumJS COPC Runtime is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically
constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and restrictions on geospatial datasets.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and help repair harm.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and sexualized
or demeaning conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `ysm287@gmail.com`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, code review, project events,
and public representation of the project.
